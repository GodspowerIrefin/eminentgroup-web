Registered Nurse

MEDICAL PROFESSIONALS, AT YOUR SERVICE

Registered nurses for clinical, hospital, home, and residential settings.

Registered nurses monitor patient health, make sure treatments and medications are given as planned, and keep doctors informed of changes. Their duties differ by facility, and we match each RN to the setting that fits.

What RNs do

  • Assess, plan, and deliver nursing care to accepted standards.
  • Give treatments and medications and monitor how patients respond.
  • Keep detailed records of patient progress.
  • Coordinate care with other health professionals and answer patient and family questions.

Where we place RNs

  • Hospitals
  • Nursing homes
  • Rehabilitation centers
  • Assisted living facilities
  • Home care agencies
