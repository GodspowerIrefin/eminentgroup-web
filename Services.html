QUESTIONS

Frequently asked questions.

What settings do you staff?

Hospitals, nursing homes, rehabilitation centers, assisted living facilities, home care agencies, and other healthcare settings.

What staffing terms do you offer?

Temporary, temp-to-perm, and permanent placements, short-term or long-term.

How do you verify professionals?

We check licenses, certifications, employment history, and background checks before placement.

Do you help professionals find work too?

Yes. Apply on our For Job Seekers page and we will match you to opportunities that fit your skills and availability.

What happens after a placement?

We stay in touch with the facility and the professional to keep the placement working.

How do we get started?

Send a staffing request or call 410-929-9388. Tell us the role, shift, and start date.
